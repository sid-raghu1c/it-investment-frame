Important Instructions

Clause 6 | MP Tourism Policy, 2025

Please read and accept the following mandatory instructions before proceeding to the Capital Subsidy Application Form (Annexure – 1):

  • 1
    All documents must be valid at the time of application.
  • 2
    Copies of all documents submitted must be digitally self-attested.
  • 3
    The Madhya Pradesh Tourism Board may seek any additional documents from time to time, as required.
  • 4
    The application form must be completely filled in and all required documents must be attached with the application.
